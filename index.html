Investing in young people

AAL+ Youth’s mission is to empower young people. We create opportunities to learn, share, connect and, most importantly, take action.

We believe that by investing in youth, we can shape a better, more inclusive future for all. Our main goal is to identify and address the challenges of our age, especially those that affect young people directly.

Founded by students, rooted in a school with history

AAL+ Youth was founded by a group of high school students from Ankara, Türkiye. Our school, Ankara Atatürk High School, has a rich history dating back to the 1880s and nurtures students in an environment focused on academic and personal development.

Its many clubs and communities give young people a place to build social and intellectual skills. Many of us also organised and took part in TEDxAnkara Atatürk Lisesi, which brought our team closer and made our teamwork stronger.

Educating students since 1886

Together with Ankara Atatürk High School

As AALplusYouth, we carry out our activities in collaboration with Ankara Atatürk High School. By bringing together our school’s academic and cultural background with the energy and creativity of young people, we aim to develop meaningful national and international projects.

Through this collaboration, we create opportunities for students to turn their ideas into reality, take initiative, develop new skills, and engage with different cultures.

We believe that this partnership goes beyond institutional cooperation. It represents a shared vision of empowering young people to become active individuals who contribute to their communities and create positive change.

Our Erasmus+ journey

Our journey in international youth projects began with the development of an Erasmus+ KA152-YOU youth exchange project last year. Although the project was not selected for funding, the process provided us with valuable experience in project development, international cooperation, and youth participation.

Building on this experience, we are now taking our next step with an Erasmus+ KA210 project in collaboration with our school, Ankara Atatürk High School. This new initiative reflects our commitment to strengthening international partnerships, promoting learning opportunities, and turning young people’s ideas into meaningful projects.

For us, every experience is an opportunity to learn, improve, and move forward.
